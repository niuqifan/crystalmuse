Appearance & Natural Character

The beads appear glossy black in normal light. Under a strong flashlight, some stones may reveal warm golden-brown or translucent tones. Smaller bead diameters generally transmit more light, while larger beads may appear more opaque.

Color, sheen, inclusions, internal lines and small surface characteristics vary naturally. You can request current product photos before approval.

Care Guide

  • Keep away from chemicals and prolonged moisture.
  • Wipe gently with a soft dry cloth after wearing.
  • Store separately to reduce scratching.
  • Avoid impacts against hard surfaces.

Traditional Meaning

Obsidian is traditionally associated with grounding, boundaries and reflection. Its deep black polish creates a simple and versatile jewelry style.

Traditional crystal meanings are cultural and spiritual associations, not medical claims.
