Natural Rose Quartz Color and Clarity

Rose quartz naturally ranges from very pale blush to a fuller soft pink. Translucency, clouding, internal lines, inclusions and small color differences are normal features rather than identical factory-made patterns.

How to Care for Rose Quartz Beads

  • Remove before bathing, swimming, sleeping or sports.
  • Keep away from perfume and household chemicals.
  • Wipe gently with a soft dry cloth after wearing.
  • Store separately and avoid impacts or prolonged strong sunlight.
