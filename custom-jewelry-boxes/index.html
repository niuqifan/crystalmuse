BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Packaging questions,
answered.

Can I put my own logo on jewelry boxes?

Include your logo artwork and preferred placement in your inquiry. The suitable printing or finishing method depends on the box surface and artwork, and should be confirmed in the quote and sample.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the structure, dimensions, material and branding. Send your estimated quantity so we can check a suitable packaging option. There is no single MOQ for every design.

Can I request packaging without ordering jewelry?

Yes, you can send a packaging-only inquiry. Tell us what the box will hold and provide dimensions or a reference. Feasibility, MOQ and pricing are confirmed after review.

Can I check a sample before a bulk order?

Request a sample plan with your quote. Confirm sample availability, charges and timing before proceeding, then approve the physical fit, artwork and finish before authorizing bulk production.

How long does custom packaging take?

Sampling and production timing depend on the design, materials, quantity and artwork approval. Include your preferred delivery date and destination. Ask for sampling, production and shipping estimates separately.

Are gift boxes enough for shipping?

A presentation box is not a substitute for a shipping carton. Discuss outer packing and protection for your jewelry, and include transit requirements in the packaging brief.
