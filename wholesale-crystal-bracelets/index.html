How a bulk bracelet order works

  1. Share the brief: send target stones, bead sizes, quantities, packaging and delivery market.
  2. Confirm the offer: receive available grades, current product photos, unit pricing and lead time.
  3. Approve the sample: review a physical sample or detailed photo and video confirmation when appropriate.
  4. Production and QC: bracelets are assembled, measured, checked and counted by SKU.
  5. Export packing: cartons are prepared for international transit and dispatched with tracking.
