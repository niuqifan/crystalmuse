Buyer Questions

Wholesale crystal bracelet FAQ

What is the MOQ for wholesale crystal bracelets?

Typical MOQ starts around 50–100 pieces per style. Trial availability depends on stone, size, grade, packaging and stock.

Which gemstone bracelets can I order in bulk?

Common options include Amethyst, Rose Quartz, Clear Quartz, Tiger's Eye, Black Obsidian, Citrine, Green Aventurine and mixed chakra bracelets.

What bead sizes are available?

Popular wholesale sizes include 6mm, 8mm and 10mm. Other sizes depend on gemstone and stock.

Can orders include retail-ready packaging?

Yes. Options can include bags, pouches, meaning cards, barcode labels, gift boxes and export cartons.

How are bulk bracelet orders checked?

Checks can cover bead size, color range, visible defects, polish, circumference, stretch cord, knot, quantity and packing.

Do you ship wholesale orders worldwide?

Yes. Shipping is quoted by destination, carton weight, order value and required delivery time.
